# Commercial Credit-Hold Policy

## Policy Objective
This document outlines the governance criteria, credit limits, and shipment release thresholds for commercial credit accounts.

## Credit-Hold Triggers
An account is automatically placed on Credit Hold if any of the following conditions occur:
1. **Delinquent Invoices:** Any single invoice remains unpaid for more than thirty (30) calendar days past its due date.
2. **Credit Limit Breach:** The total outstanding invoice balance exceeds the approved customer credit limit.
3. **Disputed Charges:** Unresolved invoice disputes exceeding $5,000 pending audit review.

## Operational Impact
When an account is in Credit Hold status:
- All pending and processing warehouse orders are immediately blocked from packaging and shipping.
- Fulfillment allocations remain reserved for a maximum of 5 business days before release to general inventory.
- Sales representatives cannot create new orders with credit terms without Finance approval.

## Release Procedures
Shipment holds can only be lifted upon:
- Full settlement of all past-due invoices via verified payment receipt.
- Explicit written approval and credit line temporary extension signed by the Finance Controller or CFO.
