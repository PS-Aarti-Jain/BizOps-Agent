# Supplier Service Level Agreement (SLA) & Vendor Governance

## Policy Objective
Establishes performance metrics, lead time standards, and supplier ranking criteria for procurement.

## Performance Metrics & Quality Scores
1. **On-Time Delivery (OTD):** Approved suppliers must maintain an On-Time Delivery rate of at least 95%. Deliveries arriving more than 2 business days after promised lead time incur penalty credits.
2. **Defect & Return Rate:** Defect rate must not exceed 0.5% per batch. Defective shipments require immediate replacement within 3 business days.
3. **Reliability Score:** Computed on a 0.0 to 1.0 scale based on historical compliance, order fulfillment rate, and invoice accuracy.

## Vendor Selection & Ranking Hierarchy
- **Cost vs Speed Trade-Off:** The procurement engine ranks suppliers primarily by total landed cost for requested quantities.
- **Lead Time Premium:** If warehouse inventory is critically depleted (stock = 0), a higher-priced supplier may be chosen if lead time is significantly faster, provided the price difference does not exceed 10% without managerial sign-off.
